You make the music. We'll handle the files.

Lazy Creatives finds every project you've made, backs it up, matches it to the songs you exported, and posts those songs to SoundCloud without doubles. It runs on your own computer, needs no account, and never changes your projects. The jobs you stop doing ↓

BackupsLazy Creatives

Free beta

Stop digging through folders and copying projects by hand.

Browse every project you've made in one library: every Ableton, FL Studio, Logic Pro, Studio One, Bitwig, Reaper and Audacity project on your computer, searchable by name, tempo or genre, with the songs you exported ready to play.

Back them up when you're ready. It follows each project's samples, copies a complete deduplicated snapshot to a drive, NAS, Dropbox or Google Drive folder you choose, then re-reads every file to prove the backup opens.

Also shows which songs came from each project, which samples are missing and which plug-ins your projects use.

What's new in Backups 0.1.7

3 October 2026

New

  • Search and filters in the Library. Type part of a project name, a genre or a song title and the list narrows as you type, even with a typo. Filter by backup state, DAW, genre, tempo range, or whether a project has a song (or one on SoundCloud). One click on Clear all shows everything again. In the Crate look, click any column heading to sort the list by it.
  • Check for updates yourself. The bottom of Settings shows which version you have and a Check for updates button. It tells you straight away if you are up to date, or offers the new version.

Fixed

  • FL Studio samples no longer show as missing when FL Studio plays them. If a project still remembers a sample's old spot (another computer, a network address or a different user name), Backups now finds the same file in your own folders, the same way FL Studio does.

What's new in Uploader 0.1.0

4 October 2026

New

  • Post your mixes to SoundCloud without ever posting the same one twice. The app knows what's already up, even when the same mix is saved in another format.
  • Your tracks: edit, hide or delete the tracks already on your SoundCloud from one place.
  • Knows where each song came from. If you use Lazy Creatives Backups, each mix shows the project it was exported from, with its tempo and genre filled in.
  • Cover art made for you from the song's sound.
  • Two looks, your choice, the same as Backups: Crate or Sleeve. A song has the same cover in both apps.
  • Play your mixes in the app from a player bar at the bottom.
  • The app updates itself, and shows what's new after each update.
  • Everything is unlocked while the app is a free beta.
