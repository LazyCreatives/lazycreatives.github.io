Privacy, plainly.

No account, no tracking, nothing sent to us.

The short version

We collect nothing.

The apps contain no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads and no tracking. There's no account to make. We don't collect your email, your project names, your file list or your music, and we have no server that could store them.

When the apps go online

Three reasons, all listed here.

Checking for updates
Each app asks its own GitHub Releases page whether a newer version is out. Nothing about you or your files is sent.
Cloud mirror (optional)
If you choose to mirror backups to your own Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive, files go directly from your computer to your account. We never see them or your login.
SoundCloud (Uploader)
Your audio goes directly to SoundCloud. Signing in passes through a small Lazy Creatives service that only swaps the sign-in code for your access keys and keeps nothing. The keys are stored encrypted on your computer.

This website

No cookies, no tracking.

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages and sets no cookies. It reads the latest version notes from GitHub when a page opens. GitHub may keep its own server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.
